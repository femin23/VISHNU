00 BIOGRAPHY // AUTHOR & RESCUE OFFICER

The Mind Behind the Myth

വിഷ്ണു എം. സി — Literary & Tactical Profile

Vishnu M C is an officer in the Fire & Rescue Services (Government of Kerala), an acclaimed Malayalam author, and a dynamic scriptwriter. Combining real-world valor from public service with a vibrant creative imagination, his craft spans short stories, novels, motion picture screenplays, poetry, and reflective blogs.

His professional career in the Government of Kerala's Fire & Rescue Services equips him with first-hand experience in crisis response, tactical discipline, and frontline heroism. This real-world foundation infuses his writing with authentic tension, emotional resonance, and a deep appreciation for human resilience under pressure.

He is the author of the celebrated Kanthamalacharitham Trilogy—comprising Akhinathante Nidhi, Arolakkadinte Rahasyam, and Yuddhakandam—published by Era Books Publication, Venjaramoodu. His short story anthology Panchami Petta Panthirukulam was published by KANFED under the Kerala Bhasha Institute. Furthermore, his published short fiction includes Palayanam in Fire Service Magazine and Njan Priyadarshini Trivedi in Moolyashruthi Magazine.

01 FEATURED TRILOGY // THE BOOKS

The Kanthamala Trilogy

കാന്തമല ചരിതം — A bestselling three-part novel series blending ancient history, mythology, and thrilling adventure.

The Kanthamalacharitham trilogy emerges as an extraordinary exemplar of 'meta-fiction,' a literary genre seldom explored within the rich tapestry of Indian literature. This masterfully crafted narrative, rooted in the action-adventure genre, artfully intertwines elements of historical fiction, mythological lore, speculative science fiction, and intriguingly woven conspiracy theories. While Sabarimala—the revered Hindu pilgrimage epicentre—serves as the trilogy's spiritual fulcrum, the expansive world of Kanthamala transcends geographical confines, resonating universally across cultures and epochs. Thus, Kanthamalacharitham promises to enthral fiction aficionados worldwide with its compelling, boundary-defying narrative.

At its core, the trilogy delves deeply into the dynamic exchange of cultures. Recent archaeological revelations of the ancient Pamba Valley civilization, contemporaneous with the illustrious Indus Valley Civilization, form a fascinating backdrop. The text illuminates the enduring trade and cultural symbiosis between ancient Egypt and early Malayalam societies, tracing connections over three millennia. In shedding light on these obscured historical corridors, Kanthamalacharitham offers an unparalleled portrayal of Egyptology rarely observed in Indian literature.

This literary opus unearths a hidden visage of ancient Egypt, distinct from the narratives popularized by works such as Max Allan Collins’ The Mummy series. Central to this exploration is Amenhotep IV, or Akhinathen—whose radical monotheism and enigmatic persona, often named him as the 'Alien Pharaoh' by modern theorists, catalysed seismic upheavals within Egypt's millennia-old religious hegemony. The saga of blood-soaked conflicts against entrenched priesthoods, juxtaposed with riveting sci-fi elements like time travel, superhuman prowess, and the fusion of Indian and Egyptian mythology, delivers an electrifying reading odyssey.

Equally compelling is the trilogy’s immersion into the Sangham-era Tamil landscape. Mirroring the historical grandeur of Kalki Krishnamurthy’s Ponniyin Selvan, it meticulously chronicles the epic power struggles among the Chera, Chola, and Pandya dynasties. Iconic figures such as Jatavarman Sundarapandyan, Veerapandiyan, Rajaraja Cholan, and Chera monarch Godaravivarman animate the narrative, elevating the trilogy’s historical authenticity. This meticulous depiction ensures its robust appeal across South Indian states like Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana. The profound spiritual resonance tied to Sabarimala and the Ayyappa faith further augments its nationwide allure, positioning the English translation for imminent success.

Moreover, the trilogy adeptly integrates Erich von Daniken’s provocative 'Ancient Astronaut' theory from Chariots of the Gods with indigenous myths and spiritual paradigms. Cutting-edge science fiction motifs—LiDAR scanning, advanced exosuits, humanoid robotics, time manipulation, wormholes, interstellar voyages, and epic superhero confrontations—cater seamlessly to the tastes of anime and manga enthusiasts. The vibrant discourse generated through book reels and reviews on social media platforms underscores its magnetic appeal among Malayalam's youth readership.

Critics and readers alike draw parallels between Kanthamalacharitham and globally renowned series such as Game of Thrones, Vikings, and Shogun. The trilogy’s cinematic potential, lauded by eminent author Anand Neelakantan, attests to its expansive narrative canvas. What distinctly elevates this trilogy is its universally gratifying prose style. Comparable to the mytho-historical vibrancy of works by Amish Tripathi, Ashwin Sanghi, and Kevin Missal, Kanthamala offers a literary banquet for enthusiasts of richly woven epics. Its profound exploration of Ayyappan’s divine journey—from mortal saviour liberating a nation to venerated deity—imbues the narrative with both spiritual gravitas and captivating storytelling.

In addition to its mythological and historical prowess, the trilogy courageously interrogates caste dynamics and socio-political marginalization, amplifying voices of dissent against systemic injustices. Vivid character arcs explore youthful rebellion and societal transformation, rendering the work both timeless and contemporary. An audiobook has been released as a spin-off of the Kanthamalacharitham. Many celebrities, including renowned television actress Aswathy Sreekanth, have lent their voices to this audiobook. Acclaimed by premier publications such as Malayala Manorama, Mathrubhumi, Kerala Kaumudi, and Deshabhimani, the trilogy has garnered widespread critical and popular acclaim. It boasts impressive ratings—3.92/5 on Goodreads, 4.6/5 on Amazon, and an exceptional 4.76/5 from 421,000 listeners on Kuku FM, cementing its status as Malayalam’s premier audiobook experience.

✦ Shared Literary Universe Era Books Publication Multi-Author Crossover

One World. Multiple Writers. Interconnected Destiny.

Era Universe is the first shared universe in Malayalam literature, bringing together multiple writers to create one interconnected world of stories. Unlike traditional standalone novels, Era Universe works as a collaborative storytelling platform, where different authors contribute their own unique narratives while staying seamlessly connected within a single universe.

Characters, events, and ancient mysteries cross over between stories, gradually revealing a larger unified storyline. Blending dark fantasy, horror, deep mythology, dystopian survival, and archaeological science fiction, Era Universe grants each writer creative freedom while maintaining a strong canonical spine. Published by Era Books Publications, Era Universe stands as a multi-author cinematic universe in literature, with plans to expand into feature films, web series, anime, and visual storytelling.
